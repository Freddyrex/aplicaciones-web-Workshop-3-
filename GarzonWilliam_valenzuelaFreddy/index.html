Explore the Yachay Tech Campus

Yachay Tech University is a public research institution located in the Hacienda San José in Urcuquí, Imbabura, Ecuador. Founded in 2014 as part of the Yachay City of Knowledge project, it is dedicated to interdisciplinary scientific research and technological innovation. The name "Yachay" comes from the Kichwa word for "knowledge." The campus spans across a historic hacienda that once housed a sugar mill, now transformed into a modern hub for education, with facilities including the Sala Capitular, the Patrimonial Library, student residences, and cutting-edge laboratories. Explore our interactive map to discover every faculty, laboratory, and student space across this unique campus.
